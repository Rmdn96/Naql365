## Method and evidence limits

This is a repository-based interface and interaction audit of the merged baseline, supported by the accepted [manual-pricing report](reports/Naql365-Manual-Pricing-Coverage-Report.md). Inspected shared shells/tokens/primitives, public landing, auth/onboarding, request wizard/summary, quote actions/details/Sales, payments/proof/configuration, tracking/progress, Operations and Driver components, route entry points and existing E2E coverage.

Evidence below distinguishes source-confirmed behavior from proposed improvements and browser checks still required. Prior 269 unit/integration, 32 local E2E and eight hosted groups establish functional regression evidence; they do not establish exhaustive visual acceptance across every authenticated page. No fresh customer, staff, Finance or Driver fixtures were created in this analysis. No new screenshot audit of all Naql365 authenticated states is claimed.

The owner supplied [Naqlk](https://naqlk.vercel.app/) as a usability benchmark. On 2026-10-07 its public Arabic homepage was inspected through the browser DOM and rendered viewport. No login, submission or business action was performed. Its private dashboards, mobile layout and backend behavior were not inspected.

## Benchmark interpretation

Naqlk's public page makes its origin/coverage boundary explicit near the main message; separates request, WhatsApp and tracking actions; explains human Sales review in short steps; and groups services, coverage and support links. These are useful standards for clarity and findability, not proof of its operational authority or accessibility.

For Naql365, adopt those usability objectives while keeping its own route-line/node motif, SA/EG selector, manual-review journey and product-oriented visuals. Do not copy Naqlk's logo, truck image, copy, layout, city catalogue, Saudi-only assumptions, registration rules or tracking authorization. In particular, its visible city list is not authorization to activate those cities in Naql365. Avoid transferring unsupported marketing claims.

## Brand and interaction contract

Keep Deep Navy `#0B1F33`, Electric Blue `#1677FF`, Cyan `#22C7E8`, and Background `#F5F7FA`. Retain the existing accessible action/link blue (`#075BCB`) where contrast requires it, plus semantic success/error colors. Do not use cyan as small body text merely for branding. Alexandria and Inter are already configured with font-display swap in `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx`; no new font purchase or binaries are needed.

Use one scale for typography, spacing, border, radius and elevation, with explicit public versus dense-workspace variants. Current shared radii are 6/10px while `.launch-page` uses 20px; this is a source-confirmed inconsistency, not a reason to round every table or input to 20px. Proposed review direction: 16–24px major cards, smaller controls, restrained shadows and route motifs only at useful progress/confirmation points. Preserve logical CSS properties and locale-specific typography.

Every screen should answer: where am I, what is the authoritative state, what is my next permitted action, what happens after it, and how do I recover? Customer wording remains request, quote, payment, preparation, transport and delivery. Internal revision IDs, job aggregation and finance predicates stay out of customer explanation.

## Implementation boundaries for owner review

No schema or migration is proposed for visual polish; all 46 remain immutable. No change to backend state machines, RBAC/RLS, token authorization, manual pricing, tax derivation, currency, payment clearance, provisioning rules or assignment authority is justified by this analysis.

Allowed future work, if separately approved: shared tokens/primitives; presentational shells/navigation; localized labels/help; safe route-preserving locale links; state feedback/focus; responsive layouts; and UI regression tests. The staff logout dependency needs a narrowly scoped session/UI fix using existing trusted authority, reviewed explicitly rather than hidden as styling.

Any newly discovered requirement for broader queries, a new RPC, database write access, commercial policy or authentication redesign must be separated for review. Preserve automated pricing for future use and never reintroduce preliminary monetary data in MANUAL mode, including hidden DOM/SSR/JSON/analytics.

# Final proposed V1 schema specification

This refines [Wave 6A](../03-schema-specification.md) for Egypt/EGP, registered checkout and incremental implementation. All names/types are design proposals; no DDL or database execution is supplied.

## Structural contract

All new records have UUID `id`, `organization_id`, UTC `created_at`; mutable aggregates additionally have nonnegative bigint `revision`, `updated_at` and restricted actor references. PK is `id`; every tenant table also has UNIQUE `(organization_id,id)`. Market-scoped tables have `market_id`, UNIQUE `(organization_id,market_id,id)` and composite FK `(organization_id,market_id)` to the existing Market identity. Currency-bearing parents expose UNIQUE `(organization_id,market_id,currency,id)`. Every child FK carries the parent's organization/Market/currency where applicable; do not rely on globally unique UUIDs for tenant isolation.

The server-configured storefront organization and an existing active EG Market derive authority. V1 Store command guard requires country EG and currency EGP, independent of locale/public preference cookies. No hardcoded Market/supplier UUID. An organization-scoped supplier cannot be attached through a different tenant's offer. Existing tables remain unchanged; use referenced unique keys already present or prove equivalent immutable FK + tenant validation before later DDL approval. Missing required existing composite keys is a migration-review blocker, not permission to alter Transport now.

RESTRICT deletion of referenced commercial/financial records. No cascade from Customer/product/supplier/account deletion into orders or postings. Mutable drafts may be explicitly removed by a guarded command; financial history is append-only/reversible. Timestamps from actors are evidence occurrence times; server timestamps remain authoritative audit times.

## Entity consolidation and minimum boundaries

The complete proposed V1 has **31 relations**, delivered incrementally, not all in migration 48. This count includes durable execution, allocation and dedupe records needed for safe manual money operations. It excludes existing identity/Market/geography tables and generates no stock, supplier-Auth, guest, analytics, notification or GL subsystem.

Physical partial delivery/return facts use typed `store_events` fields for context item FK, positive quantity, event kind, occurrence time and authorized evidence binding, in addition to the primary aggregate discriminator. Validate the item belongs to that fulfillment/order; event quantity sums cannot exceed ordered/delivered net units under group/line locks. Do not mutate immutable order-item quantity to represent delivery progress or keep unrestricted quantities only in free-form JSON. The context item is not a second alternative aggregate owner. Quantity/status projections reconstruct from authorized events; group DELIVERED/RETURNED requires the corresponding complete physical facts, independent of payment.

## Why these boundaries are retained

Embed the placed delivery/contact snapshot in the order instead of a one-row address table; operational corrections are versioned events. Merge cancellation/return headers into typed cases, not their separate financial consequences. Merge manual refund/settlement execution infrastructure while keeping exclusive parent FKs. Use one typed private event relation instead of one audit table per resource. Do not add guest identities/capabilities to registered-only V1. A small versioned policy relation shares approval mechanics but each policy kind has a validated schema and different privileges; it is not a free-form authority store.

Each item belongs to one fulfillment supplier; no extra fulfillment-item join in V1. Future mixed-supplier carts can create multiple groups and separate immutable lines per source. Splitting units of an already placed line requires a separately reviewed allocation extension/version; it must not rewrite old line supply identity. Keep stable group IDs in APIs from V1. No future compatibility claim permits silent supplier substitution.

## Arithmetic and immutable snapshots

EGP minor unit scale is 100; use bigint integer amounts with checked numeric intermediates, not floating point. Proposed technical ceiling per stored nonnegative amount is JavaScript MAX_SAFE_INTEGER `9,007,199,254,740,991`; multiplication/sums must stay within range before converting to JSON numbers. Signed ledger deltas use the corresponding bounded range. Business quantity/order-value caps remain owner-approved policy; absent required caps blocks checkout. Technical representability is not a sales limit or inventory promise.

For each line: gross = quantity × approved retail unit; taxable basis, inclusive/exclusive tax decomposition and fee tax classification come from the approved tax policy. Freeze gross, discount, net-exclusive subtotal, tax, delivery allocation and total; total = net-exclusive subtotal + tax + allocated delivery charge after that charge's approved tax treatment. Order sums must equal line sums exactly. Do not derive a tax amount/rate from missing configuration or assume invoice compliance. Model policy outputs explicitly to prevent double-counting tax on delivery.

Proposed proportional allocations use largest remainder with stable `(line_number,id)` tie-break, and integer numerator/denominator calculations. Partial-return credits reverse original per-unit/line allocation slices so cumulative credit cannot exceed the original allocated amount. Approve inclusive-tax, rounding, discount funding, nonrefundable charges and fee allocation rules before writing calculators. No Transport rounding change, automatic FX, commission or statutory revenue assumption.

Freeze SKU/content/quantity, customer money, private cost, supplier/agreement/offer, policies, payment responsibility and selected destination version at placement/selection as appropriate. Separate immutable facts from mutable state/revision. Later suspension changes future eligibility, not past commercial snapshots. Corrections require reasoned approved deltas/reversal references.

## Indexes and cross-row invariants

Use unique organization/SKU, localized slug, version keys and business references. Index catalog `(org,market,publication,category,sort,id)`, customer history `(org,market,customer,created_at DESC,id DESC)`, staff queues `(org,market,state,created_at,id)`, supplier ledger `(org,market,supplier,currency,group,effective_at,id)` and every child FK. Bound page size to proposed 1–50 (default 20); cursor keyset includes sort timestamp/ID and filter context. Arabic search normalization/index choice needs measurement; no external search service assumed.

Approval intervals and selected version constraints prevent conflicting prices/offers; never pick an arbitrary latest row. Deferred checks/transaction locks enforce item/order/group totals, V1 one group, whole-quantity allocation and financial reservations. No CHECK with subqueries pretending to enforce a cross-row sum. Lock aggregate headers before reading/summing children. Unique source references supplement command idempotency. Detailed lock/retry contracts are in [commands](03-command-api-contracts.md).

Order status, fulfillment status, payment/review, physical return, refund and settlement remain independent. CLOSED requires terminal physical outcomes and resolved customer monetary obligations; later disputes append new work, not delete closure history. Payment projections distinguish cancellation of an unpaid attempt from refunds of actual received money.

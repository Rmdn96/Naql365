## Risk register

| Risk                                             | Severity | Failure / impact                                                                          | Required mitigation and acceptance evidence                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| R01 — merchant/collection eligibility unresolved | Critical | Live collection or invoices issued under wrong legal entity/purpose                       | D01–D04 signed off; per-Market fail-closed activation and configuration readback                                                     |
| R02 — supplier fails despite no stock limit      | High     | Paid order cannot be fulfilled                                                            | Real exception/cancellation/refund cases, supplier escalation duty, no fake inventory promise or mandatory pre-checkout confirmation |
| R03 — wrong cost exposure                        | Critical | Customer/Support sees private agreements or commercial margins                            | Separate cost relations, explicit projections, RLS/REST/SSR/analytics/export leak tests                                              |
| R04 — duplicate or racing money execution        | Critical | Double refund/settlement, overallocated receipt or payout after uncertain response        | Durable dedupe + source-reference uniqueness + locked allocations + independent connection races and reconciliation                  |
| R05 — COD custody confused with bank funds       | High     | Naql365 pays supplier twice or labels supplier-reported cash bank-verified                | Custodian-specific verified events, balance equation tests, hold on unverified/short collection                                      |
| R06 — returns after settlement                   | High     | Old settled record overwritten, wrong supplier recovery or duplicate refund               | Immutable reversal/adjustment ledger and worked post-settlement examples; separate customer refund from supplier recovery            |
| R07 — tenant/Market/currency confusion           | Critical | Cross-organization reads or foreign-currency debt offset                                  | Composite FKs, explicit canonical context, no FX default or cross-currency settlement; forged-context negatives                      |
| R08 — Transport coupling                         | High     | Store affects execution clearance, guest grants or sent Quote snapshots                   | Separate Store aggregates/scopes; byte-identical baseline and full Transport regression at future implementation gates               |
| R09 — unbounded quantity/search abuse            | High     | Overflow, expensive queries or supplier order abuse                                       | Approved technical caps, integer bounds/rate limits, keyset pagination; caps are not inventory                                       |
| R10 — unsafe proof/media                         | High     | Private files exposed or malicious uploads rendered                                       | Purpose-bound private Storage, limited file types/size, image normalization, safe PDF download; independent access/expiry tests      |
| R11 — Store guest capability escalation          | Critical | Reference-only access, cross-Transport authority or recovery takeover                     | D08 plus independent namespace/resource grants, revocation/expiry/replay and no cross-domain acceptance tests                        |
| R12 — authority hidden only by sidebar           | High     | Operator or Support calls money/cost RPC directly                                         | Destination, command and RLS enforcement; restricted-role tests, not only SUPER_ADMIN screenshots                                    |
| R13 — unsupported promises/translation           | Medium   | Fake ETA, stock, supplier receipt, or proof interpreted as payment                        | Approved bilingual event/copy dictionary and actual-state visual matrix; no fabricated dashboards                                    |
| R14 — migration/configuration conflation         | High     | Schema upgrade silently publishes Store, seeds live accounts or changes Transport history | Separate future schema approval and explicit owner-controlled activation; fresh/populated upgrade equivalence checks                 |

These risks do not block reviewing the architecture. They block the corresponding implementation/launch stages until resolved and tested. No current application defect is inferred from a future design risk.
